Best teas and pies: Milky for one cup

Teas and pies tried in every combination with the Buff Planner's calculation. Four rankings, for the amount gathered, short runs, fights, and per ingredient.

Answer

For one cup, Milky Pure. If you have it again whenever it ends, Creamy Pure with a rich Bear Pie

For one cup, Milky Pure is first in value × time for every kind of gathering. For its 15 minutes, the Bear Pie multiplies the effect by 1.5 (rich 1.75) and makes the time run out twice as fast, so one cup gives a little less in total. If you have the tea again as soon as it ends, the faster time does not matter, and Creamy Pure with a rich Bear Pie is the strongest for any run from 15 to 60 minutes. Per ingredient, Basic is far ahead.

Try your own combination in the Buff Planner (or open it from a name in the tables)
  • ×0.75value × time during the 15 minutes of a Bear Pie (rich ×0.875)
  • +87.5%average ore bonus (Creamy Pure and a rich Bear Pie, had again)
  • 64berries in one Pure tea (Basic takes 4)

Every ranking tries all the combinations with the calculation of the Buff Planner (the stacking rules read from the server's code). Only one effect of a kind works, so two ore teas cannot be stacked.

For its 15 minutes, the Bear Pie makes ore, wood, scrap and harvesting 1.5 times as strong (rich 1.75), and their time runs out twice as fast. Value × time during those minutes is 1.5 ÷ 2 = 0.75 (rich 0.875). A harvesting tea ends within the 15 minutes, so it loses that much; ore, wood and scrap teas last longer, and the rest runs at the normal speed, so the difference is smaller (0.83 to 0.96 between the tops of the tables). For one cup, you always gather more without it.

The strongest set is Creamy Pure Max Health Tea (max health 120), a rich Pork Pie (health over time at 3.5 a second), a rich Hunters Pie (clotting) and Creamy Pure Anti-Rad Tea (−45% radiation damage). Advanced is made from 4 teas, and Pure from 4 Advanced, so one Pure takes 16 Basic teas (64 berries). Value × time only doubles for ore and harvesting, and grows 3 to 6 times for wood and scrap, short of 16 times the ingredients, so Basic gives the most per ingredient. With berries to spare, Pure or Milky Pure lasts long; with few berries, several Basic teas gather more.

How it is worked out

  • The values and times are the data exported from the server (build 2026-11-07). How effects stack and how the Bear Pie works were read from the server's code.
  • +40% for ore, wood, scrap and harvesting means what you gather, or the scrap of a crate, is multiplied by 1.4.
  • The tables are worked out by content/columns/buff-ranking.py, and a check makes sure the tables of this column are the same.
