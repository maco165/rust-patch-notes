Craft PlannerGame build 2026-11-07

Enter the explosives to make, what you hold and your furnaces, and it shows the materials you are short of and the time to smelt them. It can take over the way you picked in the Raid Planner.

You enter
The explosives to make, the materials you hold, the kind and number of furnaces
You get
Sulfur ore still to mine, wood to gather, what is short per material, and the furnace time

Buff PlannerGame build 2026-11-07

Put teas and pies in the order you have them, and it shows how strong the ore, wood, scrap, max health and other effects are, and until when.

You enter
Teas and pies, and when you have each
You get
The value of each effect and when it ends, time bands, and the calories and water you get at once
Rules
How effects stack, read from the server’s code. A later effect of the same kind takes the place of the earlier one, and the Bear Pie makes the gathering effects stronger but shorter
Open the Buff Planner

TTK PlannerGame build 2026-11-07

Pick the armour and its inserts to see the protection of each body part, and the hits to the head, torso and legs for every gun and bow, with the damage falloff by distance.

You enter
An armour set, the kind and number of inserts, and the distance
You get
Protection per body part; hits, damage per hit and time to kill per weapon; hits every 10 m
Rules
The damage formula read from the server’s code. Only the clothing and inserts covering the hit area protect, added up; the head takes 2×, the legs 0.75×
Open the TTK Planner
