How it is counted

The counts are worked out from the game server’s data and code, not counted by breaking things in the game.

Explosives 8 notes
  • Each target is counted on its own. Splash from a rocket onto the wall next to it is not counted.
  • Mixing two explosives on one target (finishing a C4 with ammo, for example) is not counted.
  • Deployables (Auto Turret, SAM Site) are counted with HV (High Velocity) Rockets that hit, whatever way is picked. Whether you can get near a live turret is not counted. A SAM Site blocks most of that rocket’s damage, so its count is high.
  • C4 and satchels are counted stuck to the target: all the damage goes into it and none into the wall or door beside it.
  • Rockets and grenades are counted exploding right at the target. Further away the damage drops and the count rises.
  • A blast meets the building’s side protection from any side. The side only changes the count for explosive 5.56 shot at a wall.
  • Explosive 5.56 is counted from an Assault Rifle within 20 m. Other weapons differ.
  • A satchel is a dud 20% of the time, a beancan 15%. Duds can be picked up and lit again, so the count is the same but you light more often.
Materials and furnaces 5 notes
  • Sulfur and materials are counted back through Explosives, Gun Powder and Beancan Grenades. Server settings (damage multipliers) are taken as default.
  • One sulfur ore gives one sulfur. A furnace is as fast however the ore is split over its slots: a Furnace smelts 0.6 a second, a Large Furnace 3, an Electric Furnace 1.
  • A fuel furnace burns one wood every 2 seconds whether or not it is smelting, and a wood leaves 0.75 charcoal on average (75%), so the wood is an expected value. An Electric Furnace burns no wood: charcoal is counted as made in fuel furnaces.
  • “Both in the same furnaces” smelts the ore in fuel furnaces and takes the charcoal from the wood they burn: both are done after the longer of the two times.
  • The times are for a server that is not lagging. On a heavy server a wood burns longer, so wood and charcoal can take longer than shown. The ore time does not change.
