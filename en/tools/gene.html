    How it is counted

    The results are worked out from the game server’s code and data, not tried by planting in the game. The rules come from the research note on crossbreeding and gene effects (in Japanese).

    Crossbreeding rules 6
    • Crossbreeding happens once, at the moment the middle sapling enters the Crossbreed stage. There is no luck involved.
    • For each gene position, the weights of the surrounding saplings’ genes (X and W 1.0; G, Y and H 0.6) are added up per gene, and that position takes the gene whose total goes above the weight of the middle sapling’s gene there. A tie does not change it.
    • When totals tie, the gene that reached that total first in planting order wins. Plant in the order shown and you always get this result.
    • Only living plants of the same kind in the same planter within 1.5 m count as neighbours. The middle of the large planter has 8 neighbouring slots, the middle of the small one 2, and every slot of the triangle planter 3.
    • If a surrounding sapling crossbreeds before the middle one does, its own genes change. Plant the middle sapling first and let it grow ahead.
    • After a server restart, the order they are counted in may differ from the planting order (not checked).
    Reading from images and the screen 5 notes
    • Images are read only inside this browser and sent nowhere.
    • It reads a row of six white letters on green and red circles (in a sapling’s description and on a planted crop). The circle’s colour tells good genes (G, Y, H) from bad ones (W, X), and the letter’s shape settles which. The old display with coloured letters and no circles cannot be read.
    • The letter shapes were taken from published screenshots of the game. A circle cut off at the edge of the image or an unclear shape becomes “?”: fix it before adding.
    • Reading the screen, it reads the shared screen every 0.5 s and adds a sapling once all six letters read the same twice in a row. While the display stays open, the same sapling is not added twice. Close it for a second or more and show the same sapling again, and it is added as another one: take it out if you do not want it.
    • Screen sharing works in browsers on a computer (Chrome, Edge and others). The shared screen is neither recorded nor sent. Reading goes on while the browser is behind the game; if it stops, keep the browser on another display.
    The search and the effects 4 notes
    • Every choice is tried: one of your saplings in the middle and, from the rest, up to as many as the planter has neighbouring slots around it. Only one crossbreed: plans that reuse a crossbred sapling are not shown.
    • In a gene position where totals tie, the planting order decides which gene wins. Every combination of winners over the tied positions is checked, and those that some planting order produces are shown.
    • “Off the target” is the number of letters that would need to change. Ties are ordered by fewest bad genes (W, X), then fewest saplings.
    • Growth speed goes up 25% per G and yield 25% per Y. Every two Y give one more clone. Each H raises the temperature range by 0.05, and each W makes the plant take 10% more water.
