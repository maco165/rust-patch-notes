How it is counted

  • Materials are counted back through Explosives, Gun Powder and Beancan Grenades.
  • The time is furnace time only. Crafting time at the workbench and the time to mine and gather are not included.
  • One wood leaves 0.75 charcoal on average. The wood is an expected value.
  • Metal Fragments are counted as they are. The metal ore and its smelting time are not included.
  • The times are for a server that is not lagging. On a heavy server wood and charcoal can take longer.
